# What the plan cannot know

- **Whether the estimates are right.** The plan adds up the days in the file. A wrong estimate gives a wrong plan with the same confidence.
- **Whether the work splits across people.** Capacity is people times days, as if every feature could be shared. Two people do not build one small feature twice as fast, and a larger team needs time to coordinate.
- **Time that is not building.** App store review, a security questionnaire from a customer, holidays and waiting for a third party are not in the days unless you put them there.
- **Value.** The 1 to 5 scale is a judgment. The plan uses it only to order the later releases, and lists the features where it had to assume 3.
- **What users will do.** The later releases are a starting order. After the first release, what users actually do should reorder them.
