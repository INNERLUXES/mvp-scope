# 1. The estimates come from the reader

## Context

A planning tool is tempting to fill with default estimates: sign-in is 3 days, payments are 5. Those numbers are wrong for most products, because they depend on the stack, the design, the rules the data falls under and the people building it. A default estimate also looks like knowledge when it is a guess.

## Decision

- The tool has no built-in estimates. Every day in the plan comes from the file.
- The example file is marked as an example, and its numbers only show the shape.

## Consequences

- The plan is only as good as the estimates, and docs/limits.md says so.
- The feature list becomes a document in its own right, which can be versioned and reviewed next to the design it estimates.
